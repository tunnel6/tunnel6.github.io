## What is Edge

Your NAS, your database and the dev server on your laptop all sit inside a private network, so nobody from the outside can reach them. Edge fills that gap. It runs on a machine that has a public address and keeps one long-lived connection to your local YAT client, authenticated in both directions with mTLS. An outside request lands on Edge first and is then handed to your service over that connection. You open no port on the internal machine and change nothing on the router.

```
Without Edge:  visitor ✗ -- the router has no port forwarding, the request cannot get in
With Edge:     visitor → Edge (public entry) → authenticated connection → internal service
```

## What Edge is responsible for

| Responsibility | Details |
|----------------|---------|
| **Public entry** | Listens on HTTP/HTTPS, TCP, UDP and WireGuard ports and takes incoming traffic |
| **Forwarding** | Routes a request to the right tunnel by domain or port; the response returns over the same connection |
| **Identity and encryption** | Mutual authentication with Captain and with clients end to end, so trust comes from identity rather than network location |
| **Domain assignment** | Each tunnel gets a system-assigned hostname such as `abc123.edge.myroxy.dev`, or one of your own |
| **Status reporting** | Reports online state, tunnel list and traffic statistics, which the console uses for health checks |

## Where an Edge comes from

| Source | Details | Fits |
|--------|---------|------|
| **Official nodes** | Provided by YAT across several regions, reachable as soon as you register an account | Most personal and team setups |
| **Self-hosted Edge** | Deployed on your own VPS; the bandwidth, ports and certificates are yours alone | Production, or when you need to control the egress |
| **Shared Edge** | An Edge shared by your other devices or by someone else, which you subscribe to | Testing, occasional use |

Self-hosted and shared nodes are both managed on the **Edges** page of the client; the steps are in [Deploy Edge Server](./deploy.md) and [Edge Marketplace and Sharing](./market.md).
